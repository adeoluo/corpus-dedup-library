# Data Card - Corpus Dedup Library

## Source
- **Fully synthetic.** Documents are generated in-process from a seeded word pool. There is no
  external dataset, download, or network access.

## Composition
- Distinct documents are random word sequences of 14 to 20 words.
- Near-duplicates are produced by editing a chosen document: an exact copy, an appended clause, a
  changed first word, or a prefixed phrase. The mapping from each duplicate to its source is the
  ground truth for detection.

## Labels and supervision
- The only labels are the injected near-duplicate relationships, used to score detection precision
  and recall. Deduplication itself is unsupervised.

## Provenance and licensing
- Generated by this repository's code under the MIT license. No third-party text is included.

## Filtering and contamination
- Not applicable: the corpus is constructed with known duplicates on purpose, so detection can be
  measured against ground truth.

## Known limitations
- The synthetic corpus is small and clean apart from the injected duplicates, so it demonstrates the
  mechanism rather than the messiness of real web text.
